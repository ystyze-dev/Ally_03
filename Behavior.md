## Behavioral cycle
1. Listening - Escuchar
The fan listens to the subject's discourse in english or spanish. 
2. Interpreting - Interpretar
The raspberry Pi recognizes heat-related vocabulary and accumulates linguistic heat. 
3. Giving - Dar
The fan provides airflow proportional to the amount of recognized heat vocabulary. 
4. Withdrawing - retirarse
when the subject stops speaking about heat, the blades of the fan gradually stop rotating. 
5. Desiring Desear
During silence, the fan generates poetry expressing its own need for air, proximity and breath. 
6. Acknowledging - agradecer
The fan recognizes the breath and responds with a disipating effect on the poetic words displayed on the raspberry Pi. 
7. Reconnecting - reconectar
The fan returns to listen mode, and the reciprocal cycle begins again.
