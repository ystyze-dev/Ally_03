A breeze shaped by your words.

Aera imagines the fan as an extension of the person speaking: heat in your words gathers into a breeze, and a poem arrives when the conversation falls quiet.

The breeze recedes

When heat-related words stop, the simulated fan steps down through three speeds and comes to rest. A quiet pause invites the poem.

A poem in the pause

When the speaker falls quiet, Aera offers a brief poem about heat. Speaking again returns attention to the conversation and the fan response.
